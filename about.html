About Better Than UR Daddy’s Automotive

A mobile automotive diagnostic & repair service built on honesty, skill, and a commitment to doing the job right — the first time.

Our Story

BTUD started with a simple idea: bring professional automotive diagnostics and repair directly to the customer. No towing. No shop delays. No guessing. Just real answers, real repairs, and real service — wherever you are.

Founded in Augusta, GA, Better Than UR Daddy’s Automotive is built on years of hands‑on experience, advanced diagnostic training, and a passion for helping people stay safe on the road. We believe in transparency, accuracy, and treating every vehicle like it’s our own.

Whether it’s a check engine light, electrical issue, brake job, or a full diagnostic workup, we bring the tools, the knowledge, and the professionalism of a shop — straight to your driveway.
